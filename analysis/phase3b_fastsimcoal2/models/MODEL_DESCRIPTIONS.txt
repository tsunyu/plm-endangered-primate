TIME CONVERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

fastsimcoal2 uses units of GENERATIONS.
To convert to YEARS: years = generations × 10

Examples:
  TBOT = 130 generations = 1,300 years ago
  TBOT = 500 generations = 5,000 years ago

PARAMETER INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ne values:
  - Reported as diploid effective population size (not census size)
  - Typically Ne < Nc (census size)
  - Ne/Nc ratio often 0.1-0.5 for mammals

Likelihood values:
  - Use MaxEstLhood for best-run selection and AIC comparison
  - Do not use MaxObsLhood to rank independent optimization runs

Growth rates:
  - Negative values = population expansion
  - Positive values = population decline
  - Rate is per generation
