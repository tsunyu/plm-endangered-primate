================================================================================
WHY EXCLUDE SEX CHROMOSOMES?
================================================================================

1. DIFFERENT EFFECTIVE POPULATION SIZE
   - Autosomes: Ne
   - X chromosome: Ne = 3/4 × autosomal Ne
   - Y chromosome: Ne = 1/4 × autosomal Ne
   - Mixing produces BIASED estimates

2. DIFFERENT INHERITANCE
   - Autosomes: Diploid in both sexes (2 copies)
   - X: Hemizygous in males (1 copy), diploid in females
   - fastsimcoal2 assumes autosomal diploid inheritance

3. STANDARD PRACTICE
   - Nearly all published demographic studies use autosomes only
   - This is the accepted method in the field
   - Important for peer review and publication

================================================================================
TECHNICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

fastsimcoal2 Model Assumptions:
  - Diploid organisms (2 copies per individual)
  - Random mating between individuals
  - Equal inheritance from both parents
  - Consistent effective population size (Ne)

Sex Chromosomes Violate These:
  - X: Not diploid in males (only 1 copy)
  - X: Spends 2/3 of time in females (not equal)
  - X: Different Ne (3/4 autosomal)
  - Y: Only in males, no recombination

Result: Including sex chromosomes produces biased Ne estimates

Solution: Use autosomes only (standard practice)
